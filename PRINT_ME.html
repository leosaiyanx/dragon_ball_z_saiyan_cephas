Five things worth knowing

  • Press attack from far away and you fly at them automatically. You never have to walk into range.
  • Hold the punch button — punch, punch, kick, punch, spin kick, then press SMASH to launch them into the sky.
  • Hold CHARGE until the blue bar fills. Specials and transformations both cost ki.
  • Fire a beam into an incoming beam and they lock together. Mash attack to shove the ball of light into them.
  • Tap GUARD at the exact moment a hit lands and you teleport behind your attacker.
On a phone or tablet: scan the code, then use Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or ⋮ → Install app (Android) to get it as a real app icon that works with no internet.
Gamepad: plug one in and it works — A punch, B ki blast, X guard, Y charge, D-pad specials.
Two players: pick 2 Players for split screen on one keyboard.
No internet at all: use the single file dist/SaiyanCephas-standalone.html from the repository.
